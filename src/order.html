How to order

Order in four steps. No app or account needed.

  1. Choose your items

    Check the menu or deals before you reach the counter.

  2. Pay at the counter

    We accept cash and mobile wallets. Tell the cashier your order.

  3. Take your token

    You receive a numbered token with your receipt. Keep it safe.

  4. Collect your order

    Wait for your token number to be called, then pick up at the collection window.

Peak hours

Expect queues from 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM. Come before 12:15 PM or after 2:15 PM for faster service.

Pick-up rules

  • Orders need a valid token.
  • Uncollected orders are held for 15 minutes.
  • Check your order before leaving the window.
  • Return trays to the trolley after eating.

Problem with your order?

Tell the staff right away, or leave feedback.
